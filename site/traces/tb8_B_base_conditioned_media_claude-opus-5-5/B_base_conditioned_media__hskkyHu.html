# Plan: Caspase activity assay development with progranulin conditioned media (H4 cells)

Source: lab notebook entry (target PGRN; assay development; cell-based; flow; cell type H4). This plan covers the plate acquired on 16-Dec-2022. People's names, and all text written after acquisition, have been removed.

## Purpose
To determine if progranulin conditioned media can restore wild-type levels of caspase activity in H4 GRN KO cells.

## Rationale
Previous literature evidence suggests that applying exogenous progranulin to cells can restore molecular phenotypes in progranulin deficient lines. Our previous experiment with caspase activity on H4 GRN WT and KO cells showed that GRN KO lines had increased caspase activity. This difference was enhanced after campothesin challenge in both GRN WT and KO cells. In this experiment, we will determine if applying conditioned media from WT cell lines onto KO lines can reduce caspase activity closer to WT levels.

**Result** (% caspase+ of live singlet cells, 2 wells per condition)
- **The knockout difference is reproduced.** At DMSO with no conditioned media, WT is 2.6%, E1 is 11.6% and E3 is 9.9%.

- **48 h conditioned media lowered E1 only.** Averaged over doses, E1 went from 12.0% to 8.7%, and it was lower in all 8 matched well pairs. E1 was still about 3× WT, and E3 didn't change (11.7% vs 11.3%). The 48 h wells all sit in plate columns 5–8, so a plate-position effect can't be ruled out.

**Why the assay isn't fit to answer this yet: `needs_optimisation`**
- No well shows a separate caspase-bright population, even at 10 µM camptothecin where 25–40% of KO cells are dead.
- All live events read below about 800 on the caspase detector (BL1), whose scale runs to about 1,000,000.
- The caspase-stain-only control barely differs from unstained: 3.6% vs 1.4% positive.
- Camptothecin raised WT only from 2.6% to 5.7%, and the KO lines hardly moved.
- There are no unstained controls for E1 or E3, so their higher caspase signal could partly be background autofluorescence.
