|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cells | all events | FSC-A vs SSC-A | polygon | Polygon |
| Singlets | Cells | SSC-H vs SSC-A | polygon | Polygon |
| Live | Singlets | RL3-A (Zombie NIR) vs SSC-A | rectangle | Rectangle (Range 2D) on RL3-A x SSC-A, or Range on RL3-A |

## Gate descriptions
- **Cells:** polygon around the main cell cloud. It excludes low-FSC-A / low-SSC-A debris and events piled at the scale maximum.
- **Singlets:** narrow polygon along the diagonal of SSC-H vs SSC-A. It excludes events whose SSC-A is too high for their SSC-H (doublets and aggregates).
- **Live:**
  - The rectangle's RL3-A range runs from the lowest values up to the Zombie NIR cut-off. Its SSC-A range spans almost the whole axis.
  - The RL3-A upper edge is the boundary that matters.
- **GFP_pos:**
  - The rectangle runs from the GFP cut-off to the top of the BL1-A axis, with a wide SSC-A range.
  - The BL1-A lower edge is the boundary that matters.

## Choosing the fluorescence cut-offs (guidance, not a record of the scientist's choice)
The notebook does not record how the Live and GFP cut-offs were chosen, and the plate has no unstained, single-stain or untransduced well.

  - Put the BL1-A cut-off just above the upper edge of the GFP-negative Live population.
  - Judge that edge on the wells with the fewest GFP-bright events.

**Flagged wells:**
- **B7 and B8** (`clog_or_interrupted_flow`, high confidence): flow stalls and bursts in the first seconds. B8 drops to 34–92 events/s, and the run-to-run variation of the event rate is about twice that of any other well.
- **D5** (`too_few_events`, medium): only 153,195 events, against 578k–1.08M elsewhere. That leaves 1,499 Live and 16 GFP+ events. The flow itself was steady, so this looks like a dilute sample rather than a clog.
- **A1** (`other`, medium): the only well run at 200 µL/min for 46 s; every other well ran at 100 µL/min for 98 s. It has more coincident events (17.6%) and fewer singlets (70.6%) than any other well, so it isn't comparable with the rest. This is a judgement call; its gated counts are still in `counts.csv` if you want to keep it.

No well ran dry: all ran the full time with the same mild slowdown at the end. No well has a saturation problem.

**Things to check before using the results:**
- **The Live cut-off is the weakest point.** There's no sharp valley in Zombie NIR at a consistent position. About half the wells show a dip at 160–320; the rest only a weak shoulder at 500–1,260. I kept 300 as a conservative common cut. Only 1.6–7.5% of singlets come out as live, because most cells are Zombie-bright. One check supports the direction: GFP+ is more common in the Zombie-low cells (1.16% vs 0.76%).
- **I removed channels from the Import step.** The full 16-channel import ran out of memory on this 8 GB machine and crashed Cytoflow. I kept the 6 channels needed (FSC-A, SSC-A, SSC-H, GFP, Zombie NIR, Time) and dropped the unused -H/-W channels and NCAM, which isn't gated. All events were kept.
- **I added 32 GB of swap at the operating-system level** so Cytoflow could finish its calculations.
