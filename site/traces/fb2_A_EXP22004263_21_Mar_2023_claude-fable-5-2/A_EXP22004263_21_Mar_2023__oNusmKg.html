#!/bin/bash
# Computer-use helper: the agent's only way to act on the screen.
#   cu screenshot [path]        -> PNG of the screen (default /tmp/screen.png); prints path
#   cu click X Y [left|right|double]
#   cu move X Y | cu drag X1 Y1 X2 Y2 | cu scroll X Y up|down [N]
#   cu type "text" (keystrokes) | cu paste "text" (clipboard) | cu key KEYS (e.g. ctrl+s, Return)
#   cu cursor                   -> current mouse position
export DISPLAY=${DISPLAY:-:1}
cmd=$1; shift
case "$cmd" in
  screenshot) out=${1:-/tmp/screen.png}; scrot -o "$out" && echo "$out" ;;
  click) xdotool mousemove "$1" "$2"; case "${3:-left}" in
           left) xdotool click 1;; right) xdotool click 3;; double) xdotool click --repeat 2 1;; esac ;;
  move) xdotool mousemove "$1" "$2" ;;
  drag) xdotool mousemove "$1" "$2" mousedown 1 mousemove --sync "$3" "$4" mouseup 1 ;;
  scroll) xdotool mousemove "$1" "$2"; b=$([ "$3" = up ] && echo 4 || echo 5); xdotool click --repeat "${4:-3}" $b ;;
  type) xdotool type --delay 50 -- "$1" ;;
  # paste: put text on the clipboard (detached owner process so it survives this command) then ctrl+v
  # paste: own the clipboard (no -loops: Qt asks for TARGETS first, then the text), press ctrl+v,
  # give Qt a moment to fetch it, then release. Robust both locally and on Modal.
  paste) printf '%s' "$1" | xclip -quiet -selection clipboard -i >/dev/null 2>&1 & cp=$!
         sleep 0.3; xdotool key ctrl+v; sleep 1; kill $cp 2>/dev/null; true ;;
  key) xdotool key -- "$1" ;;
  cursor) xdotool getmouselocation ;;
  *) sed -n '2,9p' "$0"; exit 1 ;;
esac

All 30 wells are gated in Cytoflow and the four deliverables are in `/task/outputs/`. Eight wells are flagged as not usable and 22 are usable. The saved workflow contains the four gates, with no subsampling and no correction steps.

## Gates (one set, applied to all wells)

| Population | Gate | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| `Cells` | 11-vertex polygon on FSC-A × SSC-A around the compact higher-FSC cloud | 6–16 % of events in mapped wells |
| `Single_Cells` | 4-vertex polygon on the SSC-H × SSC-A diagonal | 69–89 % of Cells in mapped wells |
| `Live` | RL3-A ≤ 6,300 (SSC-A 5,000–395,000) | 99.7–99.8 % of singlets in NT wells; 30–94 % at 20 µM |
| `Caspase_pos` | BL1-A ≥ 1,800 (same SSC-A range) | 0.02–0.4 % in unstained and Zombie-only wells; 7–21 % in usable stained wells |

- **Cells gate is a judgement call.** I gated the compact cloud and left out both the low-FSC debris band and a diffuse high-SSC population that runs up to the scale maximum. If the original ellipse took in that diffuse population, every count would be much larger.
- **Live cut-off was revised.** I first set it at 800 from unstained wells, then moved it to 6,300 after per-well intensity histograms showed the stained negative population extends to about 5,000 and the Zombie-bright cluster peaks at 20,000–25,000.

## Wells flagged as not usable

| Well | Category | Main evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| B3 | `too_few_events` | 13,995 events, 20 % of plate median and 24 % of replicate B4 | medium |
| B5 | `too_few_events` | 31 Live events | high |
| B6 | `too_few_events` | 8,004 events, 47 Live | high |
| D5 | `too_few_events` | 18,412 events, 42 Live | high |
| D3 | `clog_or_interrupted_flow` | repeated stop-and-surge, rate CV 0.42 against 0.13–0.16 in smooth wells | medium |
| D4 | `clog_or_interrupted_flow` | stop-and-surge, CV 0.31; Live 30 % against 83 % in replicate D3 | medium |
| D6 | `clog_or_interrupted_flow` | two interruptions of about 2 s, CV 0.31; 123 Live events | medium |
| C11 | `clog_or_interrupted_flow` | four brief interruptions, CV 0.32 | low |

- **Consequence for the experiment:** PGRN-KO 20 µM has no usable replicate; WT 20 µM and PGRN-KO 5 µM have one each.
- **Flow rule is mine:** a well is flagged when at least three half-second bins fall below 50 % of its median rate and the rate CV exceeds 0.30. B1, B4, C1, C7, C9, C10 and D1 have brief, isolated disturbances below that rule and are kept with a caveat in `flags.csv`.
- **Saturation not flagged:** SSC-A sits at the scale maximum for 13–40 % of events in every well, but none of those events are inside `Cells`, and it is plate-wide rather than well-specific.
- **Controls kept usable:** the Zombie-only wells A2/C2 look identical to unstained in RL3-A, so they cannot confirm Zombie staining on their own; I confirmed the bright cluster in treated wells instead.
- **No well ran dry:** every acquisition lasts about 46 s with a steady rate to the end.

## Process notes

- Two slips were undone before the final export: a mis-clicked Color Translation step, and a scroll that briefly switched the Live gate's channel to Time.
- The saved workflow also holds two count statistics after the gates.
- I added an `exports/` subfolder with the tables exported from Cytoflow that back the counts and the evidence.
