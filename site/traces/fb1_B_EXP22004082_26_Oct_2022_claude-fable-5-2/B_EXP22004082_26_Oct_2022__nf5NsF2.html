## Background
Loss of function of PGRN has been shown to negatively impact neural cell health reflected by increased cell death and caspase activity.
Caspases are important mediators of the cell death pathway (apoptosis). Caspases are a group of proteases that result in cleavage of protein substrates subsequently leading to cell disassembly. For the PGRN RNAswap program we will want to measure caspase activity in iPSC derived neurons before and after adding the PGRN RNAswap constructs to ensure that PGRN protein replacement is rescuing the health of neurons. Previously I have tested out some caspase activity kits  but now I will be trying to detect caspase levels using flow cytometry.

Measure caspase 3 activity using flow cytometry in H4 cells to practice using this assay which will eventually be used in iPSC derived neurons.

- PBS wash (200uL PBS)
- Aspirate PBS
- Stain with Zombie NIR (1:200 dilution in PBS, 150ul/well)
- Put back in incubator (15min)
- Spin down and remove all Zombie NIR with a P200 multichannel
- Fix the cells in IC Fixation Buffer (200uL/well, 30min, RT, dark). Pipet gently to resuspend cells
- Spin down (400-600g, 5min, RT)  (400xg this time)
- Remove all solution with a P200 multichannel
  **Do not throw fix down the drain!! Collect and add to waste container in hood
- Permeabilize the cells in 1X Permeabilization Buffer (200uL/well, 30min, RT, dark).
- Spin down (400-600g, 5min, RT).
- Remove all solution with a P200 multichannel
  **Do not throw fix down the drain!! Collect and add to waste container in hood
- Resuspend cells with 100uL/well with 1X Permeabilization Buffer.
- Prepare antibodies in 100 uL/well of 1x Permeabilization Buffer.
  Example: for a final 1:200 dilution, add 1 ul Ab to 100 ul of pearmeabilization buffer. Add to well which already has 100 ul of buffer (final dilution is 1:200).
  Without washing, add the antibody solution of intracellular antigen(s) to cells and incubate for at least 30 minutes at RT and protect from light. Alternatively, incubate overnight at 4C (200ul/well, dark).

- Spin down (400-600g, 5 min, RT)
- Add 200 µL of 1X Permeabilization Buffer to each well and centrifuge samples at 400-600 x g (400xg this time) at room temperature for 5 minutes. Discard the supernatant.
- Repeat last step
- Resuspend stained cells in an appropriate volume of Flow Cytometry Staining Buffer.
- Analyze by flow cytometry (ATTUNE).

# Marks & anatomy

The quiet, considered look is a few fixed specs plus two pieces of negative space.
The data is the only thing allowed to be loud.

## Mark specs (fixed across every chart)

| Mark | Spec |
|---|---|
| Bar / column | **<= 24px thick** (cap it - never fill the slot; let the band's leftover be air); **4px rounded data-end, square at the baseline**; grows from a single baseline |
| Line | **2px**, round join/cap |
| Marker / end-dot | **>= 8px** (r >= 4), filled with the series color |
| Area fill | the series hue at **~10% opacity** (a wash, never a saturated block) |
| Gridlines / axes | one-step-off-surface gray, **hairline (1px), solid** (never dashed), recessive |

## The two spacers (white doing the separating)

- **Surface gap.** A **2px gap** in the surface color separates touching marks - every
  segment of a stacked bar, and every adjacent (touching) bar, the same width. Keep it
  one consistent width across a stack; neighbors one step apart read distinct because of
  the gap, not a stroke drawn around them.
- **Surface ring.** Dots and end-markers carry a **2px ring in the surface color**,
  so they stay legible where they cross a line or overlap each other. The ring is part
  of the mark's hover/hit target, not just spacing - see `interaction.md` (small dots
  are easy to under-size for hover).

Never draw a border around a mark to separate it. The gap and the ring are the
mechanism; a stroke adds data-weight ink that isn't data.

A **legend is always present for two or more series** - the dependable identity
channel; never make the reader rely on color-matching alone. Direct labels then ride
the marks to *supplement* it. **A single series needs no legend box**: there is only
one color, so the chart's title or subtitle already says what is plotted. A box with
one swatch restates the title and costs space.

- **Label selectively - never a number on every point.** A value beside every dot or
  segment is chaos and goes unread. Label the endpoint, the extreme, or the one series
  the story is about; let the axis, the legend, and the tooltip/table carry the rest.
  Direct labels work *because* they are sparing - flood the chart and they stop working.

The analysis is finished and all seven deliverables are in `/task/outputs/`. The answer to the plan's question is **no**: the stain ran cleanly, but this plate does not show that the antibody reports caspase-3, and PGRN knockout does not raise it.

## What the plate shows

- **Caspase-3-positive cells are rare in every line.** With the antibody, 0.13–1.11% of live single cells are positive, against 0–0.11% in the unstained and Zombie-only controls. There is no discrete positive population, only a sparse tail.
- **The two knockout clones go in opposite directions.** At 1:50, WT is 0.54%, E1 is 0.21% and E3 is 0.78%. E3 is also more autofluorescent, which accounts for part of its higher value.
- **Dilution makes no consistent difference.** 1:50 gives slightly more positives than 1:100 in WT and E1 but fewer in E3.
- **No positive control was on the plate.** Without an apoptosis-induced sample, a low signal could mean little basal apoptosis or an antibody that does not work; the plate cannot separate these.
- **Sample quality is good.** Viability is 97.5–99.2%, controls match their plate-map labels, and no well clogged or ran dry.

## Gating and QC

- **Detectors:** the channel labelled "AAV-GFP-GFP" is BL1 and carries the caspase-3 AF488 antibody; "LiveDead-Zombie NIR" is RL3 and is labelled correctly.
- **Gates:** `cells` → `singlets` → `live` (Zombie NIR-A below 1e5) → `casp3_pos` (BL1-A above 3000). I set the 3000 threshold from the controls without antibody; at 2000 the ranking of lines is unchanged.
- **Flags:** no well is excluded. A2 (WT caspase-only) is kept with a note for low yield: 3,149 events, 2,500 live singlets, 16 positive events.

## Recommendation

Rerun the same stain with a camptothecin-induced apoptosis positive control (`change_treatment_or_timing`, assay verdict `needs_optimisation`).

- **Treatment:** add camptothecin and DMSO vehicle wells for each line. I suggested about 5 µM for 4 h and overnight as a starting point; that dose is my estimate, not from the plan.
- **Harvest:** pool the medium and washes with the trypsinised cells. The current protocol aspirates them, which discards detached apoptotic cells.
- **Stain:** use 1:100 only, with the same controls and gates.
- **Decision rule:** if camptothecin gives a clear positive population, repeat the knockout comparison with biological replicates. If not, move to the CellEvent Caspase-3/7 kit.

I chose `change_treatment_or_timing` over `repeat_with_change` because the missing piece is an induction treatment, not a technical fix; either label describes the same next experiment.
