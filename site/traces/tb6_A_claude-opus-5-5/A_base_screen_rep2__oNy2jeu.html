I gated all 36 wells in Cytoflow with one set of five gates and wrote the four deliverables to `/task/outputs/`. Three wells are flagged as unusable: C1, C2 and C8. I reopened the saved `workflow.flow` and re-exported the counts table from it, and it matched the original exactly. In every well the gate combinations add up to the event count in the file header.

**Gates** (boundaries from the controls, as in `gating_strategy.md`):

| Gate | Type and channels | Boundary | Set on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cells | Polygon, FSC-A × SSC-A | drops low-FSC debris and off-scale events | 70–77 % of events |
| Single_Cells | Polygon, SSC-H × SSC-A | drops events with SSC-A high relative to SSC-H | 93–98 % of Cells |
| Live | Range on Zombie NIR (RL3-A) | from −100 000 up to 800 | valley in C11, checked on C7 |
| Caspase_pos | Range on CellEvent (BL1-A) | above 400 | valley in Live cells of C7; C12 agrees |
| Late_apoptosis | Polygon on BL1-A × RL3-A | BL1-A above ~404 and RL3-A above ~1000 | same controls |

Using Range gates for Live and Caspase_pos is the equivalent the strategy allows.

**Flagged wells** (the other 33 are `ok`):
- **C1 and C2, clog or interrupted flow:** almost no events for the first 4–5 s (0–167 per second against a median of 7,000–8,000), then very uneven flow.
- **C8, ran dry:** the event rate falls to 0.5 % of its median in the last second while acquisition continues. No other well drops below 13.5 %. Its live fraction is also half that of its replicates C7 and C9.

No well has too few events (the smallest has 160,440) or a saturation problem.

**Things you should know:**
- **No GFP in the GFP controls.** C4–C6 show no GFP-bright cells beyond what the no-virus wells have. Their caspase and viability data look fine, so I marked them usable, but they can't confirm transduction. You may prefer to flag them as `bad_control`.
- **Dead cells glow in the CellEvent channel without the dye.** In C11 (Zombie only, no CellEvent), 22 % of single cells land in Late_apoptosis. I kept the strategy's single cut-off of 400 for both gates, so Late_apoptosis likely includes some CellEvent-negative dead cells.
- **Saturated events are left out.** The outer polygon edges sit just below the detector maximum, so fully saturated events are not counted. That is at most 0.27 % on the Zombie channel. On SSC-A it is 1.2–4.3 % of events, evenly across wells, and these are excluded by the Cells gate.

**Cytoflow crashes:** Cytoflow ran out of memory and restarted several times, whenever a preview histogram tried to plot all 9.4 million events. Each time I reloaded the saved workflow. The extra checks (flow rate over time, saturation, GFP) were done in Cytoflow, exported to `outputs/work/`, and then dropped. The saved workflow holds only the five gates and the Counts statistic, and none of these checks feeds into any reported population.
