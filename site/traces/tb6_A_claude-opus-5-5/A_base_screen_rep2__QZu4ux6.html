final result
I've gated all 36 wells in Cytoflow with one gate set and written all four files to `/task/outputs/`. The 180 counts come from Cytoflow Count exports, and the per-well All_events total matches the event count in every FCS header. Four wells are flagged as unusable (C1, C2, C8 and C9 is not among them; see below), and the main open question is where the caspase cut sits.

**Gates** (raw data, each drawn on its parent populations)
| Population | Gate | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Cells | Polygon, FSC-A × SSC-A | debris cut at FSC-A ≈ 7e4; 57–66% of events in every well |
| Single_Cells | Polygon, SSC-H × SSC-A | follows the singlet diagonal; keeps 93–98% of Cells |
| Live | Range on Zombie NIR (RL3-A) | −1e6 to 1000, the valley between negative and bright cells in C11 and C7–C9 |
| Caspase_pos | Range on caspase (BL1-A) | ≥ 400 |
| Late_apoptosis | Polygon, BL1-A × RL3-A | BL1-A ≥ 401 and RL3-A ≥ 1001, so it lines up with the two edges above |

The strategy allows Range gates in place of the two rectangles.

**Wells flagged as unusable**
- **C1 and C2 (clog or interrupted flow):** for the first 5 s almost no events came through (0–2% of the normal rate), then flow was erratic. The per-second rate varies by 77% and 49%, against 4–19% in every other well.
- **C8 (ran dry):** the rate falls to 16% of normal at 39 s and to zero after 40 s.
- **Not flagged:**
  - **Saturation:** 1.3–3.8% of events sit at the SSC-A maximum, about the same in every well.
  - **Event totals:** every well has 160k–419k events.
  - **Brief flow blips:** short dips, spikes or end-of-run slowdowns in C7, C9, C11, B2, A7, B8, C5, C6, B9 and B11 each affect under 3% of events. They are noted in those wells' evidence and kept.

**Judgement calls to check**
- **Caspase cut at 400.** It sits in the dip of the pooled caspase histogram. It is also where only 5% of the live cells in C11 (which got no caspase dye) would count as positive. No control shows a clean two-peak split, so this is the cut most likely to be argued about.
- **Late_apoptosis background.** Dead cells glow more in the caspase channel even without the dye. That puts 28.7% of single cells in C11 into Late_apoptosis with no caspase dye at all, against 78–92% in stained wells. Read Late_apoptosis percentages as including that background. I kept one caspase cut for both populations, as the strategy asks.
- **C10 (unstained) is kept as usable.** 32% of its RL3-low cells are above 400. That is expected: with no Zombie dye, its dead cells also land in the RL3-low gate, and C11 as a whole gives about 30%.
- **Events at the detector maximum are excluded.** Cells leaves out events at the FSC-A/SSC-A ceiling. Late_apoptosis cannot be drawn past about 1.04e6, which drops at most 0.3% of events (Zombie-saturated cells) from any well.
- **GFP wells C4–C6.** Only 0.2–0.9% of live cells are GFP-bright. I noted this but did not flag it.

The saved workflow also contains extra Binning and Count steps I used for the time, saturation and histogram checks. None of them changes channel values.
