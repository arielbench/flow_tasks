All 30 wells are gated in Cytoflow and the four deliverables are in `/task/outputs/`. 13 wells are usable and 17 are flagged. I reopened the saved `workflow.flow` and re-ran it in Cytoflow, and the exported counts matched `counts.csv` exactly.

**Gates** (one per population, the same for every well):

| Population | Gate | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `Cells` | 8-vertex polygon on FSC-A × SSC-A (log). Cuts low-FSC debris and the events stuck at the top of the SSC-A scale. | 41–56 % of events |
| `Single_Cells` | 4-vertex polygon on SSC-H × SSC-A, along the singlet diagonal | 89–95 % of Cells |
| `Live` | Range 2D, Zombie NIR ≤ 1,000 × SSC-A 1e4–1e6 | – |
| `Caspase_pos` | Range 2D, caspase channel ≥ 3,000 × SSC-A 1e4–1e6 | 0.2–1 % positive in the no-caspase-dye wells |

Cytoflow gates don't nest, so each count is the overlap of all the gates above it (for example, Live = Cells and Single_Cells and Live).

**Flagged wells:**
- **Bad controls (4):** A2 and C2 ("Zombie only") contain no Zombie-bright cells (0.03 % and 0.00 %) and behave like unstained wells. A3 and C3 ("caspase only") contain 18–21 % Zombie-bright cells, so they can't serve as Zombie-negative references. I therefore set the Live cut-off from A1/C1 plus A2/C2 instead of A3/C3; this is recorded as a deviation in `notes.md`.
- **Clogs (9):** the event rate nearly stops for at least one 0.5-s window, usually followed by a burst as it clears.
  - Repeated or long stalls: C10, C11, D1, D3, D4, D6 (high confidence).
  - A single deep stall: B1, C1, C9 (medium confidence).
- **Too few events (3):** B3 has 13,995 events, B6 8,004 and D5 18,412, against a plate median of 68,283.
- **Unexpected (1):** B5 is 98.5 % dead with no plate-map entry.

**Calls you may want to revisit:**
- **B3:** its 1,261 Live events are borderline, so it is flagged at medium confidence. Its replicate B4 is fine.
- **C1:** it is flagged for one brief stall, but I still used it with A1 to set the Live and caspase cut-offs. A stall doesn't change autofluorescence, and A1 alone gives the same boundary.
- **Not flagged:** milder slowdowns in A11, B4, C7 and D2 stayed above 25 % of the normal rate. Short end-of-run tapers in C5, D2, D4 and C9 looked like the sample running out at the set volume, not running dry.
- **Saturation:** 13–40 % of events in every well hit the top of the SSC-A scale. The `Cells` gate removes them, but this affects the whole plate, not individual wells, so no well is flagged for it.
