**Flagged wells (`flags.csv`)**
- **`bad_control` (A2, C2, A3, C3):** the single-stain controls don't contain what the plate map says.
  - The Zombie-only wells A2/C2 have no Zombie signal. Only 0.43% and 0.74% of events are bright, the same as unstained A1/C1 (0.53% and 0.75%).
  - The caspase-only wells A3/C3 do contain Zombie dye: 11.8% and 18.4% bright events, similar to the untreated double-stained wells A7/C7 (14.5% and 13.1%).
  - A2/C2 still work as caspase-negative references.
- **`clog_or_interrupted_flow` (B1, C10, C11, D1, D3, D4):** the event rate repeatedly stops and then bursts, with 7.6–22% of time bins off the local median. Clean wells have none.
- **`too_few_events` (B3):** 13,995 events and 422 live cells, against 58,782 and 3,490 in its replicate B4.
- **`mislabeled_or_unexpected` (B5, B6, D5, D6):** the four wells missing from the plate map. They are at least 87% dead-cell debris, with only 6–31 live cells.

**Judgement calls you may want to review**
- **Where I drew the clog line:** a well needed at least 7.5% of time bins off the median plus repeated stop-and-burst events. C1 and C9 each had one short stop, and A11, B4, B5, C2, C7 and D2 had small dips. I kept those wells usable because their caspase and live fractions stay stable across the run.
